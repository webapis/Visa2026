## Ädim 1 — Employees sanawy

1. Visa2026-a giriň.
2. Çep menýudan **Employees** saýlaň.
3. Sanaw ýüklenýänçä garaşyň.

![Işgärler sanawy](../../../assets/screenshots/v2026.08/tk/person-register-step-01-employees-list.png)

## Ädim 2 — Täze işgär

1. **New** basyň.
2. Işgär **detail form** açylýança garaşyň.
